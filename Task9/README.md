Internship Practice – Sales & Profit Analysis
This folder contains Excel and Tableau visualizations created during internship practice tasks. The focus is on analyzing sales and profit data from the Sample Superstore dataset.

Visualizations Included:

Top Product Sub-Categories by Sales – Bar chart and table highlighting Phones, Chairs, Storage, Tables, and more.

Profit vs Sales Scatter Plot – Compares profitability against sales across product categories.

Ship Mode Sales – Donut chart showing distribution across Standard Class, Second Class, First Class, and Same Day.

Sales by Category – Bar chart comparing Technology, Furniture, and Office Supplies.

Region Profitability – Bar chart showing profit distribution across Central, East, South, and West regions.
